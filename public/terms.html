Using this site

By booking through this site, you agree to show up for your scheduled appointment or cancel/reschedule using the link provided in your reminder text.

SMS Terms

By checking the SMS consent box during sign-up, or by checking in as a walk-in with your phone number, you agree to receive text messages from SuitesByHeadPoets related to:
