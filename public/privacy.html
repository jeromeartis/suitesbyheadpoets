Privacy Policy

SuitesByHeadPoets (Headpoets barbershop) — last updated August 2026

This policy explains what information SuitesByHeadPoets ("we," "us") collects through our booking website and text messaging service, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you book an appointment, check in as a walk-in, or create an account, we collect your name, mobile phone number, and appointment details (barber, service, date/time, and any notes you provide). If you create an account, we may also collect an email address if you choose to provide one.

How we use your information

We use your phone number to send appointment-related text messages, including booking confirmations, day-before reminders, walk-in queue updates, and account sign-in codes. We do not use your information for anything beyond operating the booking and messaging features of this site.

SMS messaging

By providing your mobile number and opting in, you agree to receive text messages from SuitesByHeadPoets related to your appointments and account. Message frequency varies based on your activity (bookings, reminders, sign-ins). Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message, or get help by replying HELP.

No sharing of mobile numbers

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in status with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. Your number is used solely to operate this shop's booking and appointment communications, and is shared only with our SMS delivery provider (Twilio) as needed to send those messages.

Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services and comply with legal obligations.

Your choices

You can update or remove your information by contacting the shop directly, or by using the account management tools on this site. You can stop receiving texts at any time by replying STOP.
