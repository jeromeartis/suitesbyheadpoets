Privacy Policy

SuitesByHeadPoets (Headpoets barbershop) — last updated August 2026

This policy explains what information SuitesByHeadPoets ("we," "us") collects through our booking website and text messaging service, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you book an appointment, check in as a walk-in, or create an account, we collect your name, mobile phone number, and appointment details (barber, service, date/time, and any notes you provide). If you create an account, we may also collect an email address if you choose to provide one.

How we use your information

We use your phone number to send appointment-related text messages, including booking confirmations, day-before reminders, walk-in queue updates, and account sign-in codes. We do not use your information for anything beyond operating the booking and messaging features of this site.

SMS messaging

Text messaging consent is collected directly on this website through a dedicated, unchecked opt-in checkbox at the point where you book, check in as a walk-in, or create an account. Consent to receive text messages is never a condition of purchase and is not bundled into any other agreement. By checking that box you agree to receive text messages from SuitesByHeadPoets related only to your appointments and account (booking confirmations, day-before reminders, walk-in queue updates, and account sign-in codes). Message frequency varies based on your activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help.

We do not share your information

SuitesByHeadPoets does not sell, rent, lease, share, or otherwise disclose your mobile phone number, your SMS opt-in, or any consumer consent information to any third parties or affiliates for any purpose. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties or affiliates under any circumstances, including for lead generation, affiliate marketing, or promotional purposes.

The only party your mobile number is disclosed to is our SMS delivery provider, Twilio, and only for the sole purpose of transmitting the appointment and account messages you have requested. Twilio acts as our service provider and does not use your number for its own purposes.

Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services and comply with legal obligations.

Your choices

You can update or remove your information by contacting the shop directly, or by using the account management tools on this site. You can stop receiving texts at any time by replying STOP.
